# Sideby design and brand

Sideby Parent is the web/mobile guardian experience. Sideby Connect is the clearly identified child companion. Working tagline: Connected families. Healthy habits.

## Visual identity

Two open, rounded links form an abstract S and represent family members side by side. Parent and Connect variants use distinct backgrounds while sharing the same mark. The vector assets are an original starter identity, not a trademark-cleared final brand.

Colors: deep teal #0F766E, navy #12313A, pale mint #E8F6F2, coral #F58A75, white #FFFFFF. Use dark text for important content; coral is an accent, not small body text. Use a system sans-serif stack initially.

## Interface principles

Provide a clear child switcher, one card per child, and device-level detail. Label demo data, timestamps, pending commands, offline states, and permission problems. Do not show green protection status without evidence. Avoid blaming language in child-facing screens.

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, responsive layouts, and WCAG AA contrast targets. Do not rely on color alone. Use large touch targets. Give destructive actions clear consequences and recovery where possible.
