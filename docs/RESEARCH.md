# Research references

Reviewed for the initial planning discussion on 23 October 2026. Vendor features and plans can change; verify again before implementation or purchasing.

- [Google Family Link](https://families.google.com/familylink/): screen-time and family controls baseline.
- [Qustodio](https://www.qustodio.com/en/features/): separate parent/child experiences and reports.
- [Bark](https://www.bark.us/): safety alerts and family management.
- [AirDroid overview](https://help.airdroid.com/hc/en-us/articles/4677178239131-AirDroid-Parental-Control-Overview): monitoring feature reference, not evidence Sideby can reproduce it.
- [AirDroid platform comparison](https://www.airdroid.com/parent-control/cross-platform-parental-control/): Android/iPhone capabilities differ.
- [Android screen projection](https://developer.android.com/media/grow/media-projection): capture authorization requirements.
- [Android foreground service types](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/service-types): camera/microphone constraints.
- [Google Play monitoring policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888380?hl=en): transparent monitoring requirements.
- [Apple Family Controls](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols): separate iPhone feasibility path.
- [Neon scale to zero](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/scale-to-zero): idle compute and automatic wake-up.
- [Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing): free inactivity pause is distinct from automatic deletion after seven days.
- [LiveKit](https://docs.livekit.io/intro/about/): media transport is separate from database storage.
